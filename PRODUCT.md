# Product

## Register

product

## Users
- **Procurement Officers**: Need fast entry, shortcuts, and efficient workflow to manage PR/PO and vendor tracking.
- **Approvers/Executives**: Need high-level dashboard summaries of budget status, and prominent, clear actions for approving/rejecting requests.
- **IT Admins**: Require rights management (RBAC) and license usage tracking.
- **Employees**: Require an intuitive, easy-to-use interface to submit procurement requests (PR) without extensive training.

## Product Purpose
The Enterprise Procurement & ICT Operations Platform streamlines the procurement workflow, approval chain, budget management, license tracking, and vendor relations for medium to large enterprises. It aims to secure official procurement pipelines, prevent budget leakage, and simplify user interactions.

## Brand Personality
- **Professional & Trustworthy**: Official, secure, enterprise-grade feel.
- **Clean & Structured**: High legibility, clear visual hierarchy, Navy base colors (`Classic Navy`) with soft slate/silver accents.
- **Efficient**: Avoids distracting elements, focusing on clarity of data and primary actions.

## Anti-references
- **No overly rounded UI elements**: Keep corner radii strict (Radius ≤ 12px) to maintain a formal, professional appearance.
- **No fancy gradients or text gradients**: Avoid decorative, low-contrast gradients that degrade legibility and look unprofessional.
- **No soft/large drop shadows with thin borders**: Avoid SaaS cliché cards with giant blurs. Use clear borders or light, crisp shadows.

## Design Principles
- **Data Clarity Over Decoration**: Prioritize raw information readability, structure, and accessible color coding.
- **Frictionless Approvals**: Action items (like approval buttons) must be clear, well-spaced, and unambiguous.
- **Strict Accessibility**: Maintain high text contrast (WCAG AA) to prevent eye strain during long working sessions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- **WCAG 2.1 AA Contrast Compliance**: Dark slate/ink text on clean light background.
- **Keyboard Friendly**: Clear focus outlines and easy navigation for rapid entry.
